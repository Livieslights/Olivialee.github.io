Behind the Lights

Hi, I'm Olivia! 💗

I'm a creative marketer and community-focused storyteller who loves turning ideas into things people actually want to see, share and connect with.

My work lives somewhere between social media, psychology, events, branding, storytelling and meaningful community work.

Throughout high school, I volunteered and participated in community service. I've continued building connections through local organizations and community initiatives.

I also partner with Volunteer Lubbock and have continued looking for ways to connect creative work with community impact.

I'm especially passionate about helping organizations feel more approachable, engaging and connected to the people they serve.

I believe good marketing shouldn't just look pretty. It should make people feel something.
